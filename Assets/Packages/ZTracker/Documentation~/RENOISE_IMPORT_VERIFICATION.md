## Warnings, suggestions and decisions

Use modern, straight-timed Renoise composition and simple sample maps; treat plugin suggestions as sound-design starting points. No plugin patch decoder ships. Unknown effects, phrases, scripts, groove, historical timing and unproven automation stay inactive with grouped issues. Older accepted LPB engines retain notes and initial tempo but not pattern commands. Refer to the guide for the exact profile.

Reimport replaces the arrangement but preserves every retained target instrument, including generated sampler targets. Consequently, changed source sample banks do not silently refresh an edited target: make a fresh comparison import and deliberately select its instrument. Names plus patch/chain evidence improve matching, but indistinguishable duplicates cannot promise stable intent after arbitrary edits. Review Changes.

Decoded samples are exact in the codec fixtures; Unity's persisted PCM path differed by up to one 16-bit step (1/32768 full scale) in the measured storage fixture. Fine tuning, loop endpoints, envelope release and mix laws still require cross-host calibration. No claim of audible equivalence is made. No player build, Test Runner, new editor, Renoise/Redux/plugin process or physical listening was used. No decision is needed from the owner; broader native conversions and patch adapters remain future measured work.
