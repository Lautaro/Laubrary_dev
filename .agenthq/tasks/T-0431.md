**Approach:** a new Step setting from instant (0) to a full glide, applied in the engine where the modifier's output is computed, not just in the interface. Choose and document whether it is a share of the step length or a fixed time.

### 2026-09-27T15:54:21Z — agent — in_progress -> done
Done. A new Step setting, 'Smooth', from 0 (jump instantly, as before) to 1 (glide through the whole step).

DESIGN: Smooth is the share of each step spent gliding from the previous value to the new one, not a fixed time. A fixed glide time would swallow steps shorter than itself, exactly the very short steps this is for, while a share keeps the pattern's shape however fast it runs. It only exists in Per interval timing, and is hidden in Per play, where each play holds a single value. It is applied in the engine, where the modifier's output is computed, so it is what is heard, not just what is drawn. If a step changes while a glide is still under way, the next glide starts from wherever the value had got to, so nothing jumps.

VERIFIED by measurement, driving the real voice's own render: two steps (0 then 1) at 96 ms each.
- Smooth 0: jumps within one control step.
- Smooth 0.5: rises in a straight line (0.25, 0.53, 0.81) and reaches 1.00 exactly halfway through the step, then holds.
- Smooth 1: glides across the whole step (0.50 at the middle, 0.96 near the end).
Seen by eye: the Smooth slider in the owner's Step panel.
NOT verified: hearing it; a built player.
