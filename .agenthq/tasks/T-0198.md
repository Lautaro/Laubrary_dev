### 2026-09-02T18:11:55Z — agent — in_progress -> done
A document with a composite generator could not be rendered at all once its canvas stopped being square, and the generator drew at the wrong scale once Pixel size stopped being 1. Both are fixed; the demo document renders again at 96x152 / pixel size 2.49 and its lost height stage is back.

The crash. A Pyre form is written for Pyre's canvas, and Pyre's canvas is square (Pyre.cs:1259-1260 - Width and Height are both canvasSize). Several forms take that literally: Arc Burst builds a square internal plane sized from the SMALLER canvas edge and then box-filters it into the target (ArcBurstForm.cs:454-456,520), which reads past the end of that plane the moment the canvas is taller than it is wide. Plasma Bloom does the same. Nothing was wrong before because a composite's bake box used to be an authored 128x128; T-0191 made it the canvas itself, which was the right change and which turned a latent assumption into a crash the first time you made a document taller than it was wide. The bridge now hands every form the square canvas its contract assumes and reads the node's box out of the middle - exactly what a hosted whole Pyre LAYER has always done (PyreLayerCompositeSource.cs:88-92). A square canvas takes the old path unchanged, so nothing that already worked moved.

The scale. The derived bake box was sized in samples, but the box is in canvas UNITS and the canvas spans 0.5*(w-1)*pixelSize either side of the origin (ShaperDocumentRenderer.cs:237-238). At your 2.49 every composite covered four tenths of the canvas it was meant to fill - which reads as 'the generator shrank', not as a unit error. Measured: the fitted box was ~0.40 of the canvas, it is now 1.01.

Neither of those should have been able to empty the whole preview, and that is fixed separately: a generator is code the shape engine does not own and cannot verify, so one that throws is now an empty node with a message on the transport status line rather than the end of the frame.

The missing height stage: restored, cause NOT established. I said in my first pass that Undo.RecordObject was the culprit - it snapshots an object's values but not its managed-reference registry, and a Shaper document is almost entirely SerializeReference graphs. Measured, it did not reproduce: neither a plain undo nor the realistic sequence (create the stage, then undo a LATER edit) nulled anything, and a save-and-reload round trip on a duplicate document grew the null count under neither API. The other candidate - a click on Remove height - leaves a byte-identical asset. A CLI save is ruled out, because that nulls every managed reference and here exactly one was lost while every node, composite and effect survived. So the undo sites all moved to Undo.RegisterCompleteObjectUndo as hardening on Unity's managed-reference-safe API, not as a proven fix, and I have not claimed otherwise in the changelog.

That leaves the real finding: authored data disappeared from your document and nothing in the asset can say how. A tool whose data can vanish without a trace will lose data again. Two cheap mitigations exist and neither is built - the window could refuse to drop a height stage without a confirm, the way AssetKit already gates Delete, or the document could keep a census of authored stages that a load compares against. Your call whether either is worth a task.

**Technical details:**
Fix 1 - PyreFormCompositeSource.Render hosts the form on a square canvas of the LARGER edge and centre-windows the node's box out of it (larger, so the picture is never shrunk to fit; what falls outside the box is discarded, a visible framing choice). Square canvases short-circuit to the original call, byte-for-byte. Fixed in the bridge rather than in ArcBurstForm/PlasmaBloomForm because Pyre is read-only for this programme AND because the assumption is Pyre-wide: the bridge covers every current and future form, and covers the effect-stage call sites (ShaperEffectStageRunner.cs:51,70, ShaperEffectPicture.cs:72) that go through the same source object.

Fix 2 - ShaperCompositeDef.FitTo/FitTree take pixelSize; ShaperDocumentRenderer passes doc.pixelSize. Bake raster stays 1 texel per sample; both clamped >= 1.

Fix 3 - ShaperCompiler.SafeRender wraps the generator call on both the single-instance and native-swarm paths: clears the raster, records ShaperCompositeDef.lastRenderError ([NonSerialized]), logs once per distinct message. ShaperCompositeDef.FirstError(root) is read by DescribeTransport ahead of every other transport message.

Fix 4 (hardening) - all seven Undo.RecordObject sites in ShaperWindow.cs / .Sections.cs / .Preview-adjacent code and PyreLayerShaperUI.cs moved to Undo.RegisterCompleteObjectUndo.
