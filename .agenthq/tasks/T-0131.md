**Details:**
This is throwaway test scaffolding for planning purposes -- it does not touch or replace anything real in the project.

How to use it: open it from the menu Laubrary > Shaper Mock (Prototype). You can turn any shape into a group (a 'Bag') and add pieces to it, then click the little arrow to step inside and edit a piece on its own -- a trail of breadcrumbs at the top lets you step back out. Turning a shape into a 'Composite' (a placeholder for a baked/generated shape) automatically hides the paint and outline controls, because that kind of shape doesn't have those in the real design -- and shows a stack of visual effects instead, which a plain shape or group never gets. Nothing extra shows up until you explicitly turn it on with a '+' button.

Not yet verified: the progress-bar strip's 'still baking' partial state was inferred from watching it fill in over a few seconds rather than caught mid-fill in a screenshot -- the finished (all green) and empty states were both directly seen.
