### 2026-09-27T13:35:19Z — agent — in_progress -> in_progress
Established what is on screen, by capturing the real Klip Editor window.
- With nothing playing and the effect expanded, the thin marker IS now drawn, at the set value (seen by eye, 1881 Hz on the Low pass cutoff). This comes from the uncommitted working-tree change; before it, nothing was drawn at all while silent.
- While the sound plays, NOTHING changes on the slider (16 frames captured across playback), even though the engine's live cutoff was confirmed by a separate readout to be at 20 Hz, a long way from the set 1881 Hz.
- Cause, found in the code: the window only keeps redrawing if its PREVIOUS drawing already saw a live value. The first drawing after pressing Play happens before the sound has actually started, so it sees nothing, concludes nothing is moving, and stops asking to be redrawn. The moving fill is then never drawn unless something else, like the mouse, happens to redraw the window. So the owner's 'I see nothing' was real, and the earlier 'done' was checked on numbers, never on screen.
- Also worth knowing: the effect's sliders only appear when its row is clicked open. In the collapsed row the owner normally sees, there is only the summary text 'Cutoff 1881 Hz~'.
- And in this particular chain the LFO's Amount is 4.0 with depth 1.0, which asks to move the cutoff by up to four times its whole range. It spends almost all its time pinned at 20 Hz or 20 kHz. That is audible and will look like the slider slamming to an end, not a smooth sweep.
Fix: the window asks the engine every editor tick whether this sound is playing, rather than relying on its last drawing.
