[[attach:20260831T171431Z_CHUNKS-EDITOR-BLUEPRINT.md]] [[attach:20260831T171431Z_r11-final.png]] [[attach:20260831T171431Z_r06-burst-off-timing.png]] [[attach:20260831T171431Z_r10-barrel-pop.png]] [[attach:20260831T171431Z_r07-left.png]] [[attach:20260831T171431Z_r08-min-size.png]]
**Details:**
What changed in the mock, in plain terms:

- Timing now tells the truth. Each part of a recipe gets its own track on one shared clock, they can overlap, and the number in the dial is the moment the part starts.
- Nothing jumps around any more. Adding a layer while scrolled down no longer throws the list back to the top; switching a part off no longer resizes the picture on the right; and the little marker you drag along the clock stays where you put it.
- Less wasted space. Every card is one to two rows shorter, because controls that used to sit alone on a line now share one.
- A recipe with only one timed part no longer shows timing dials that do nothing.
- New layers are numbered instead of all being called "New Layer".

How to see it: open Unity, then Laubrary > Chunks Mock (Prototype), and pick "Crate Smash" from the Recipes button. "Barrel Pop" is the one to open if you want to see a recipe that needs only one part and therefore shows nothing belonging to the others.

The build spec is the real deliverable and it is attached. It also lists four things the mock does NOT have and the real tool must, so nobody mistakes the prototype for a finished design — undo support being the important one.
