Done: the display was CORRECT but could not be read correctly, and one part was actively misleading. Established by comparing it with the engine's known output, not by eye.

THE OWNER'S CHAIN (read, not changed): a 3-step list stepping every 52 ms, driving the Low pass cutoff. (He changed it while I worked; the findings hold for each version.)

1. THE LANE (the line above the graph) IS EXACT. For every value the engine applies, I compared the lane with what the steps should do to the cutoff, worked out with the engine's own formula: 534 points checked, 0 mismatches. This is where the steps are visible: the cutoff line jumps between levels every 52 ms.

2. THE GREEN/RED BARS SHOW THE AVERAGE OVER THE STEPS, NOT THE STEPS. One bar reading covers 0.34 s, which it must in order to tell deep bass notes apart; at 52 ms a step, that is 6.5 steps (over two whole passes of the list). So each bar is the time-average of what the three steps do. Checked against each step measured on its own and averaged by time: every bar within 0.09 dB. Example: at 9.7 kHz, one step cuts 16 dB for 52 ms and the other two cut nothing; averaged, that is -1.6 dB, and that is what the bar shows. So a dramatic, fast stepping shows as a faint, steady dip.

3. THE 'KIND OF ANIMATES' WAS MISLEADING. 6.5 steps is not a whole number, so each reading happened to hold a slightly different mix of steps and the bars wobbled by up to 1 dB. That movement came from how the readings fell, not from the modulator. It looked like the steps and was not them.

FIXED so the display is honest and says how to read it:
- When anything in the chain changes more than twice within one reading, the bars hold still at the average (no false wobble), and the faint 'range over the play' blocks are hidden, because they would claim a band moves 1 dB when the steps really move it 16.
- The time row then reads 'âš  bars show the average of fast changes; the lane shows the changes'. Hovering it explains why: the reading length, the averaging, and that the lane is the engine's own value at its own update rate.
- The graph's hover text says the same.
- For a list that runs on its clock (Retrigger off), the lane's hover text says a real play joins wherever the clock has got to, and the dot shows where.
- Also fixed, found through the owner's first version of this chain: with a Scale binding, the step bars were drawn around 0, where Scale means 'multiply by zero'. His first two steps were negative, so the cutoff was pinned at 20 Hz for two of every three steps. Under Scale the bars now run from 0 to 2 around a line at 1 ('unchanged'), and hovering a step says so.

HOW TO READ IT, for the owner:
- The lane is 'what the parameter is doing right now'. Read fast changes there.
- The bars are 'what the chain does to each frequency', averaged over about a third of a second.
- When the âš  note shows, trust the lane for the rhythm and the bars for the overall tone.
