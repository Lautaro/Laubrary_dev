**Done, 2ba9770d.**

**Range decision.** The boost is its own stored setting on the Klip, multiplied into the source right after it is read, so the result is exactly boost x Drive.
- Drive (0 to 4), its bindings and game-code values are untouched.
- Widening Drive to 10 was rejected: it would change what existing Set and Shift bindings on Drive do.
- Restricting only the control was rejected: the engine would still clamp at 4.
- **Floor:** the control never goes below x1.0. Stored values below 1, including the 0 that old sounds load with, play at x1.

**The control.** "x1.0" to "x10.0" in the top row, dragged (one tenth per 3 px, Shift fine, Ctrl coarse), 91 steps. It has a fixed 46 px width carved from the Volume column, so nothing else moves. Undo and saving work as for the Volume range. Plays under way follow it, ramped over one control block.

**The Sound row's Drive is kept:** it is where Drive gets modulated, and its label shows the boost ("Drive x2.0").

**Measured:**
- **Kept check 29:**
  - an old sound plays at x1;
  - values are clamped to 1..10;
  - the analyser reads +9.54 dB at x3;
  - a play at x3 is x2.90 a play at x1;
  - a live change from x1 to x2 gives exactly x2.00.
- **The real control:** a 120 px drag goes to x5.0, and it clamps at x10.0; the Volume, Pitch and Chance columns do not move by a pixel.
- **No regressions:** the render still allocates nothing, and checks 20 to 28 pass.

**Not covered:**
- The waveform's combined lines draw only volume, pitch and time, and Drive has no curve there, so neither Drive nor the boost can appear on them.
- A bounce to a file (the old render path, the owner's T-0413) does not apply the boost.

**Noted while measuring:** the project file was auto-saved at 11:27 with Time and Pitch curves newly added to "Game over 4". None of my checks or probes touched that sound (verified: the check copies resolve to their own chains, and the first Klip is New Klip2), so this is presumably the owner's own editing, and it was left as is.
