Done, all three parts.
(a) Background: the curve now sits on a dark field with quarter gridlines, 'full'/'none' at the ends, and the play length. Behind an LFO's strength curve, the oscillator's own measured output is drawn faintly. The curve therefore reads as the ceiling that wobble can reach, which is what it is.
(b) Playhead: while the sound plays, an upright line marks the moment being heard, and a dot shows the curve's value there. It is timed the way the engine times it: an oscillator's strength over the play length, an envelope over the play plus its extra time.
(c) Not siloed: the analyser's modulation lanes show each parameter's final movement, which includes the strength curve. In the owner's chain, the cutoff's swing is visibly small at the start, largest at the middle where the triangle peaks, and small again at the end. Every modifier type reaches the overview the same way: through what it does to a parameter.
- Verified by eye: all three, in the real Klip Editor, with the sound playing (slowed, for the capture) at 45% through the play: the playhead line and dot on the curve, and the matching playhead on the lane.
