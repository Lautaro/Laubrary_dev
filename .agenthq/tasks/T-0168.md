### 2026-09-02T10:58:01Z — agent — in_progress -> done
A Shaper node can be made to move now. Its place on the canvas, its rotation, its size on each axis, its slant and the point it turns around are all animatable, and so are the sweep's arc, the shell's wall thickness, the softness a shape folds into its neighbours with, every primitive's own measurements, and the spread of a swarm. Each of them takes a curve, a random draw between two bounds, a step sequence or an oscillation over the document's own frames, exactly the way the star's arms and the fill dials already did. That closes the biggest hole against Pyre for anyone building an explosion: a shape can now grow, spin, drift, wipe on, or open into a shell across a run of frames, instead of holding one authored pose while only its colours change.

Counts stay whole numbers on purpose. Sweeping "how many sides" or "how many arms" over a curve pops between different shapes rather than animating one, so those two remain plain dials, and the swarm's lifetime stagger stays plain too because it decides how the per-instance clocks are drawn and animating it over those same clocks would be circular. Everything else on the list is promoted.

Documents authored before this change open with every value they had. Each block keeps its old storage, reads it once when the document loads, and seeds the new dials from it; a document saved by this build carries a mark an older file cannot have, which is what tells the two apart without guessing from the values.

The preview also gained a position handle. The selected shape's place on the canvas is a ring you drag, and one whole drag is a single undo. It follows the shape through every parent transform above it and through the preview's zoom, so a shape inside a rotated or scaled group still moves with the cursor rather than away from it. Where the position is already under a curve, the ring is shown greyed and refuses the drag, saying why - dragging there would have to overwrite the number the curve is scaled from, which reads as the drag doing nothing.

One judgment call made rather than asked: the blend's carve strength was promoted alongside its width and sharpness even though the task named only the latter two, because a shape whose two softness dials animate and whose third does not reads as an oversight in a card of three.

**Details:**
Engine facts, each checked in source:
