Design as built:
- ONE voice, sample-accurate: a dedicated loop reader runs slots 0 and 1 of the voice. The main copy wraps (crossmix 0) or, X source frames before the end, the other slot starts at start + (overshoot); the two cross-fade until the main copy reaches the end, where the other becomes main. Fade progress = incoming copy's position over X, so it is pitch-independent and both finish together. No second AudioSource, nothing from the main thread.
- Loop region = the Klip's trim points, snapped to whole frames (a region ending a hair past a frame read a clamped sample at the seam: measured -47.9 dB break, fixed to -82.8).
- Interpolation neighbours wrap around the region (a plain wrap on period-aligned material is seamless).
- Crossmix limit: half the loop (so one cycle's fade-in and fade-out cannot overlap); applied to the top of a range by the engine and the data.
- Random range: per-cycle draw = integer hash(play seed from token id, cycle number); drawn when the cycle's copy starts, >= half a loop before it is needed. Deterministic, no managed randomness.
- Fade shape (the fork, decided by measurement): neither fixed law is flat for all material. Equal-gain measured -3.1 dB mid-fade on noise; un-aligned tones dipped up to -8.6 dB. Chosen: (1) each seam's length is nudged up to +/-5 ms (whole frames) to where the two copies line up best (normalised correlation of the first <=512 frames), as sample-loop editors do; (2) the gains follow the two regions' measured correlation rho so the summed power stays 1: gOut=(1-p)k, gIn=pk, k=1/sqrt((1-p)^2+p^2+2 rho p(1-p)) (rho=1 -> equal-gain, 0 -> flat power), rho floored at -0.5 (boost <= +6 dB). Both computed once per cycle with bounded cost.
- Live edits: 4 new voice commands (loop start/end frames, crossmix min/max frames), resolved against the voice's OWN source; SapVoiceRegistry.PushLoop(klip) sends them to every voice playing the Looper.
- Release/stop: slots stop looping; running fade completes; measured: voice finished 3 ms after a release mid-crossfade.

Measured (check 16): steady tone on whole periods, crossmix 0 / fixed 0.2 s / range 0.05-0.3 s, pitch 1 and 0.7: residual vs ideal continuous tone -82.8..-84.0 dB, level flat to 0.001 dB; quarter-period-offset tone flat to 0.01 dB (fixed and range); noise mid-fade -0.09 dB (plain equal-gain -3.1); opposite-phase tone rescued by the nudge (flat); per-cycle draws in range, varied, deterministic per play, limited to half the loop; live region + crossmix edit mid-play heard and seamless; slicing invariance exactly 0 at 1/64/333/1024/4096 vs 256, ordinary AND compiled paths.

Known limit: a Looper with live speed (time-stretch) loops by the stretcher's own wrap, WITHOUT crossmix (the crossfade reader is the direct read). Filed as a follow-up if wanted.
