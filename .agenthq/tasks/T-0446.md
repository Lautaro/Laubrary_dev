## Description

Owner, 2026-09-27: "Currently the envelope is being re-animated on a loop in the visualisers, can you confirm that? Its wrong right?" His rule: if the sound is not playing, do not calculate any value from modifiers that are set to trigger or similar; only those that constantly loop and do not reset on trigger may animate while idle.

Follows T-0445, whose fix was chain-level: one free-running modifier anywhere switched idle looping on for the whole chain, and each loop was a fresh play that fired every per-play modifier again. Related: T-0434, T-0443, T-0445.

### 2026-09-27T18:50:42Z — agent — in_progress -> done
Done. CONFIRMED: it was a real bug, and it was wrong by the owner's rule.
HIS CHAIN, read from the live window: Low pass (cutoff 194 Hz), bound by an Envelope (Shift, depth 1) and by an LFO set to Always (0.12 Hz, Shift, depth 1). Because the LFO runs between plays, the T-0445 test switched idle looping on for the WHOLE chain, and every loop was a fresh simulated play, so the Envelope fired again each loop. The classification of the Envelope itself was right; the rule was applied per chain instead of per modifier.
FIX: while nothing plays, the analyser's series is taken from a copy of the chain in which every binding of a play-fired modifier (Envelope, per-play LFO or Step, Random) has depth none, which is exactly the set value in every combining mode. Lanes stay in place, flat where a play-fired modifier alone drives them. The series a real play leaves behind is dropped the moment the play ends (it would otherwise have kept looping with the Envelope in it). With nothing free-running, idle is simply still at the set values; the T-0445 one-play preview and pointing at the lane are removed, as they worked out play-fired modifiers while idle.
OTHER VISUALISERS CHECKED: the analyser window hosts the same panel (fixed with it). The live slider overlay reads only a real playing voice. Each modifier's shape drawing is a still picture of its curve, with its playhead drawn only while playing. None of them animated idle.
MEASURED on his real chain:
- idle series: Envelope depth 0, LFO depth 1; three samples a second apart drift smoothly (0.45 to 0.60, then 0.62 to 0.48, then 0.22 to 0.17 of the slider): the slow LFO alone. The Envelope-only shape (0.34 up to 0.53, back to 0.33 within the play) is absent.
- through a real play: while playing, the whole chain (lane swing 0.64); immediately after, back to LFO only.
- a copy with the LFO set to Per play: idle lane exactly flat at the set value (0.3293), classified as nothing running between plays.
VERIFIED BY EYE: his window, idle: the cutoff lane is a near-flat slowly drifting line, with no envelope bump, and the new status line 'not playing: only what keeps running between plays moves; press play to see the rest'.
NOT VERIFIED: the per-play-only case by eye in a window (measured only).
