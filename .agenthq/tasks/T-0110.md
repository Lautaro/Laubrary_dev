## Description

In the browser app 3D Shaper was built from, each layer owns a hand-painted strip of palette slots selected per pixel by a parameterisation of the shape (angle around it, or projection across it), with a reach control that at maximum covers the WHOLE shape. Crucially each slot carries a PROTRUSION - a height - added into the pixel's depth at full weight for patterned slots and a quarter weight in plain fill, so one strip paints and sculpts at once, the raised slots catch light and cast shadows, and a raised strip can win the depth test against a neighbouring layer (the divider the owner remembers). 3D Shaper reimplemented the strip, improved the parameterisation, and DROPPED the per-slot height, substituting a single fixed bump. File this as a FILL, not a border - the edge-reach control is one optional parameter, not its identity. Do this early: it is the cheapest thing that proves the whole colour-plus-height fill model.

The authoritative design is the "Shaper - the design" tab of this group's planning document (also at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\SHAPER_THE_DESIGN.md). Read it before starting.
