**Details:**
Root cause: _solo/_preSolo were instance-only fields on ZuiSectionToggleBar. Pyre rebuilds its window rarely so this never surfaced there; Shaper calls Rebuild() on nearly every edit, so the control (and its solo state) was destroyed and recreated constantly, silently dropping solo. Fix lives entirely in the shared control (Zui/Toolkit/ZuiSectionToggleBar.cs): new EditorPrefs keys .solo / .preSolo per prefsKey, written on every ToggleSolo()/RestoreNormal(), and restored (taking priority over the quick view) at the top of the constructor. Also narrowed one Shaper Rebuild() site: ShaperWindow.cs's layer-select button now calls RebuildLayerList()+RefreshSelectedLayerCards() (a new dedicated host for the selected layer's Z/Lifetime/Height/Mask cards) instead of a full Rebuild(). Other Rebuild() sites (Add/Duplicate/Delete layer, dial edits inside Height/Mask cards, Fill/Border/etc.) were left untouched -- those mutate the layer list itself or belong to sibling tasks' files, and are now safe regardless since solo survives ANY Rebuild().
