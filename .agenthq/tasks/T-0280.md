All 92 open dead-dial hypotheses from T-0279 are resolved; none is left as a hypothesis. Only ONE of them is a dial that does nothing an author can act on, and that one is now labelled rather than removed.

The headline is that the Jet family, Torch and Fireball are not full of broken dials — they are full of CONDITIONAL ones. 54 of the 92 are dials the engine reads only inside an `if`, and every variant these forms ship with defaults that shut the guard: Gout/Corona/Detonate all ship Ring count 0, Surges 0, Surge depth 0, Shock count 0, Shock amt. 0, Sweep angle 0, Tongue count 0; Torch's Barbs ships Whip amount 0, Surge jump 0, Surge glow 0, Heat lump 0, Curl warp 0; Fireball ships Arms 1. Each of those zeros silences a whole group of dials that are live controls repainting nothing, which is indistinguishable from a bug when you drag one. Worse, several come in MUTUALLY gating pairs — Surges needs Surge depth and Surge depth needs Surges, both shipped at 0 — so no single drag can ever reveal either. Every one of the 54 was measured dead at its variant's defaults and measured live the moment the engine's own guard was opened, with pixel counts, and each now says in its tooltip exactly what it is waiting for, naming the companion by the label the author sees on the card beside it.

12 more were never dead at all: T-0279's sweep perturbed ZUIValues by writing staticValue (invisible to a Curve-mode field), moved ints by a fixed +/-3 (below the noise floor on a 16..400 range), and rendered a 32x32 frame with the node's half-extent four times the canvas, giving 114-277 lit pixels. At each generator's own native frame with [Range]-end perturbation over three phases they all move the picture. Four of those 12 are Explosive Jet's flash dials, and they carry a real caution: the flash lives 0.095 of the loop, so a document of ~10 frames or fewer never samples it and the whole flash group reads dead. Nothing to fix, but worth knowing before authoring a short explosion.

2 are Orb's despeckle pair — genuinely live, genuinely doing nothing on four of the five Orb variants. Despeckle only drops pixels that are BOTH faint and isolated; measured across every variant at three seeds, Emberdrift (the default), Wisp, Coronal and Membrane never produce one at any threshold, while Voltcore yields 5-10 a frame. Both tooltips now say so with the measurement.

The remaining 24 are ramp knobs, and none of them is a dial on the Shaper card at all — ZuiReflect draws a PyreRamp or a ZuiGradient as ONE control, so the hosted-form dump never lists them; T-0279 found them because its sweep walked reflection rather than the drawn controls. They are still worth recording because they are inert wherever they ARE reachable, inside the ramp control's popover: the Kiln's own LUT bakes never read `space` (flipping it changes 0 of 1024 LUT entries in JetShade.Bake and 0 of 768 in PyreOrb.BakeLut), `cycle`/`cycleSpeed` are documented driver-only intent that changes no still sample and are read by nothing in any render path, and Torch's `bandLocked` is a UI floor on a band count the preset already sets to 7. Fixing those means editing ZUI, which is outside this card's file scope — flagged, not done.

T-0279's one flagged candidate bug, Fireball.sharpness, is not a bug: FireballSim only adds it when arms > 1, the same shape as the mirror/armMode pair T-0279 already labelled. Its second candidate, Explosive Jet's rootK, is the one dial in the whole set that is unreachable as shipped — Root takes the blast-schedule branch and returns before the burner-ring branch that reads rootK, and Detonate always has a schedule. Its tooltip already hinted at this but named the companion "Src R", which the card labels "Burn radius" — the reader could not follow it. Now it says both conditions in the card's own words.
