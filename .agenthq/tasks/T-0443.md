### 2026-09-27T17:25:58Z — agent — in_progress -> in_progress
MEASURED on the owner's real window, before any change, by reading at the same moment the value the lane's LINE shows and the value the dot and bars use:
- They disagreed in 5 of 10 samples, and WHICH samples disagreed changed from loop to loop. The owner's 'out of sync with time' is real.
- The time sat at 0.770 of 0.77 s in 3 of 10 samples: the pause at the end is real. It is a deliberate 0.4 s rest I added between loops in T-0423; it reads as a fault.
CAUSE: two separate simulations. The line was measured once, offline, as a play that starts on step 1 (and with any oscillator forced to the start of its cycle). The dot and bars came from a second, live run that joins the Step list's own clock (Retrigger is off), so it can be on any step. On top of that, the live run re-joined the clock at every loop restart, and the loop (1.17 s) and the step pattern have different periods, so the offset between the two shifted every loop.
FIX (structural): ONE pipeline. At the start of each loop, and the moment a real play starts, the modifiers are run by the engine for a whole play, joining their clocks exactly as a real play would. The line, the dot and the bars are all read from that one series. No rest between loops.

### 2026-09-27T17:55:40Z — agent — in_progress -> done
Done. All three of the owner's observations were real, each confirmed by measurement before being fixed.

1. 'THE FX PLOT WAS TOO SHORT': yes. His sound has a Reverb: 0.77 s of source, then about 1.4 s of tail during which the engine keeps stepping the modulation. The lane and the analyser's clock stopped at the end of the source (the time sat at '0.770 of 0.77 s' while the sound played on). Now the lane covers the sound's whole life, source plus the tail it actually rings for (2.2 s for his chain), with a faint mark where the source ends.
2. 'A PAUSE BEFORE IT RESTARTED': yes, a deliberate 0.4 s rest between loops (from T-0423). Removed; loops now run back to back, and each loop is the length of a real play's whole life.
3. 'NOT SYNCED, AND DRIFTING': yes, and he was right that they should share one pipeline. They did not. The line came from a fixed simulated play starting on step 1; the dot and bars came from a separate live run that joined the Step list's own clock. They disagreed in 5 of 10 samples, differently each loop.
   FIX: one series. The engine runs one play of the modifiers, joining their clocks exactly as a real play would. The line, the dot and the bars all read that one series at the same moment. It is re-taken at the start of every loop, and when a real play starts, seeded at that play's own start time.
   While a sound plays, the moment shown is the play's OWN clock (frames the engine has rendered, a second counter added beside the T-0419 one), not the wall clock, which is off by the audio's start-up delay.
FOUND ON THE WAY, fixed:
- A real playback bug: a Klip playing through the chain at any pitch other than 1 was pitched TWICE, once by the voice reading its source and again by the audio source resampling the output. Measured at pitch 0.5: both were 0.5, and every modifier's timing ran at half speed. The audio source now plays at 1 on the chain path. The owner's sound is at pitch 1, so he would not have heard it; any sound with a pitch range would have been affected.
- The previous live view ran as the real sound, so each loop advanced the real sound's remembered per-play step position. The analyser now runs as a stand-in with the same name.
VERIFIED:
- By measurement on the owner's real window, the line's value against the dot/bars value at the same moment: 16 of 16 agree at pitch 1, idle and during real plays, including in the Reverb tail. 5 of 6 at pitch 0.5; the one miss was at a step change, where the play's clock is exact only to one audio block (21 ms).
- The pitch fix: audio source 1.00 and voice 0.50 during a pitch-0.5 play (were 0.50 and 0.50).
- By eye, the owner's window over three frames: the dot sits on the line; each loop shows that loop's steps; the lane spans the whole life, with the source-end mark.
NOT verified: a human watching them side by side; hearing the pitch fix (T-0414).
