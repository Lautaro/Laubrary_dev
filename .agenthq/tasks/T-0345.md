Use case 2 (spawn Pyres and fling them) is buildable with workarounds. I built a ring of six Pyres plus a Trajectory from an empty recipe, in about 24 actions. The preview draws each blast's arc bending under gravity, and in Play mode the six Pyres flew up and out, peaked and fell.

What gets in the way:
1. The preview flies each blast only for the 'On screen' number typed on the Pyre Blast card, which defaults to 0.6 s. The Pyre really plays for 1.33 s, so with defaults the preview shows about half the real flight and the recipe's clock is too short. Fixing it means knowing to type the Pyre's length on the other card.
2. A pattern's blasts can only fly in one shared cone. 'Fling them outward from the ring' cannot be built.
3. 'Fling' is hard to find. The kind is called Trajectory, and the only 'flings' in the window is Fragment Fracture's tooltip.
4. 'Inherit burst direction' uses a dial that lives under the preview, on the other side of the window. The card neither shows the value nor says where it is.

There is also no spin for flung Pyres, and each burst rolls different directions while the preview shows one example without saying so.

11 frictions (4 MAJOR, no BLOCKER); details in WALK.md.
