**Root cause fixed, ed0a5789.** Every press on the waveform's first point became a trim drag (the same cause as T-0507). After the fix, a real double-click sequence (press, release, press with two clicks, release) on either end point leaves its value unchanged; a 1 px wobble moves it by that pixel only, as any drag does.

Double-click on a middle point deletes it; end points cannot be deleted, so on them a double-click does nothing.

**Not reproduced:** the "value of the closest dot" symptom, with synthetic events on either editor (waveform or chain cards). The measured cause was the trim takeover.
