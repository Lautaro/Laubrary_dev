## Description

Samples colours off a source sprite (à la Larder's `WareDebris` palette sampling) and sprays a configurable
number of 1-3px particles of random shape outward from the sprite's own footprint, with its own spread/speed/life
settings. **Must work fully standalone** — no fragment, no pyre, no timeline, no layering required — per the
design doc's standalone-first principle.

Gets its own collapsible ZUI section per the UI architecture (only shown when in use). ZUI-only, no raw IMGUI.

See `CHUNKS_OVERHAUL_DESIGN.md` §"Standalone modules" #1 and §"New shared primitives" (palette-sampled shower).

A Chunk recipe can now throw a shower of tiny coloured pixels that come straight out of the exploding picture's own colours, sprayed from wherever that picture actually is on screen. It works on its own -- you do not need fragments, explosions, layers or a timeline to use it.
**Details:**
How to use this: open Laubrary -> Chunks, pick or make a Chunk Spec, and switch on the "Particle Splash" section. Drop in the sprite you want the colours taken from; leave it empty and it falls back to whatever colours the burst was handed, then to the Sampled Debris source above it, and finally to plain white -- it never quietly does nothing. Not yet verified: nobody has opened the window and watched a splash fly; it has been checked by reading and by compiling only.
**Technical details:**
D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\Assets\Packages\Laubrary\Runtime\Chunks\Splash\ParticleSplashModule.cs (IChunkModule) and D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\Assets\Packages\Laubrary\Editor\Chunks\ChunkWindow.Splash.cs (BuildParticleSplash, stateKey "chunks.splash"). Fields: sprite, emitFromFootprint, count/sizePx/speed/life min-max pairs, directionDeg, spreadDeg, gravity, drag, alphaOverLife curve, seed (0 = reroll). Footprint scan collects opaque pixels ONCE per Fire, not per particle; pixel->world via the sprite's own pivot/PPU matching SampledChunkSprites. Particle art reuses ChunkSprites.Random(ppu) statically-cached shards scaled by localScale, so ZERO Texture2D/Sprite is allocated per particle and there is nothing to HideAndDontSave. Position/physics goes through ChunkModuleRunner.Move; a small self-owned ChunkSplashParticleFade MonoBehaviour rides alphaOverLife and self-destructs (legitimate: these GameObjects are created and owned by this module, unlike a pooled PyreBlastPlayer). Draw order via ctx.ApplyOrder(renderer, LayerName, i). KNOWN GAP for T-0080: the module uses its own directionDeg and ignores ctx.DirectionDeg, so a Follow Emitter cannot yet aim the spray by passing a reversed travel direction through the context -- an "inherit burst direction" option is the fix.
