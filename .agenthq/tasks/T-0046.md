Decisions taken without asking:
- **RNG: exact.** Python's `random.Random` is MT19937 + `init_by_array` over the seed's 32-bit chunks, `random()` = 53-bit from two words, `getrandbits`/`_randbelow` rejection sampling (randint/randrange/choice), `uniform`, `gauss` (Box-Muller with the cached second value). All of it is tractable, every rng call in the source is one of these, and every stream is seeded from plain ints (`seed + k` anchor, `seed*m + i` per frame, `seed*a + k*b + i//2` veils). So the geometry will be the SAME bolts, not statistically equivalent ones — `PlusPyRandom` next to `PlusNumpyRng` in the family assembly, verified against recorded CPython 3.12 values (random/gauss/randint/randrange/choice/uniform/getrandbits(40), a two-word seed). numpy's only roles are `interp`/`linspace` (deterministic, ported) and float32 planes.
- **One form, `layout` enum** (Core, Weave, Bolt, Crown, Lattice, Terminal, Cage, Stipple, Pinch, Lichten — a wrapped MiniRadio via ZuiReflect) rather than presets of dial values: the ten draws are ten different PROGRAMS (a cage of great circles and a ring of shards share no dial vocabulary), so a preset table cannot express them; each layout is its draw_* ported with its literals. Shared dials carry what every draw has (field aref/agamma, bloom, width/amp scales, the band palette, reroll); each layout's own knobs (counts, reach ranges, branch min/max, depth, roughness, ghost p/lo/hi/deep, dissolve/cool starts, its signature event) live in a nested `[Serializable]` settings class gated by `[ZUIShowIf("layout", …)]`, defaults = that draw's literals, cited per field. Canonical layout / default palette = **bolt / violet** (the task is named for it, the brief's parity list leads with it, and it is the pure-lightning draw; MANIFEST's #001 `core` is a body of light with whips — logged as the alternative not taken).
- Lengths: the draw literals are px at the source's 128 canvas; internally everything is multiplied by `s = min(W,H)/128` so the figure is the same at 64 px; when the canvas is smaller than 128 the form renders at k = ceil(128/canvas) and box-downsamples (premultiplied, `PlusSupersample.Downsample` float overload) — at 128 k = 1, exactly the source.
