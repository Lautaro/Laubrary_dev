Use case 6 is built except for the part it is really about: the interleaved layering. The recipe has a big Pyre behind, the Floating Disc cut into three flung pieces, a spray in every direction (two spray cards, because one only reaches a half circle) and a ring of small Pyres. It is saved as a break-up that fires when a duplicate of Floating Disc dies; the owner's Zoe was left untouched. It runs on its own and from the Zoe, with the pieces cut from the frame the Zoe was showing. I measured the depth of everything a real burst spawns. Depth is chosen per card, not per piece or per blast. So all fragments share one band, all small Pyres share the next, and all specks the next. Nothing can sit between Fragment 1 and Fragment 2, so 'Big pyre / Fragment 1 / Pyre 1 / Fragment 2 / Pyre 2 / Particles / Fragment 3' cannot be expressed. I read that list as top = back. Also found: adding a Layer Plan silently drops the whole burst from depth 500 to 0, the same depth as the Zoe's own body. WALK.md proposes the smallest fix for your approval: the Layer Plan becomes a drag-to-reorder Depth list that fills itself from the cards, with a Split toggle that gives each fracture piece and each pattern point its own row. Pieces are numbered clockwise from the top, the depth base is the burst's own 500, and card order stops deciding depth. Four questions for you are listed at its end.
