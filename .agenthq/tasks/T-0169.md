A Shaper swarm now has a FIGURE and a LIFETIME, which is what it was missing next to Pyre's.

Pick a spawn shape (Circle, Triangle, Square, Pentagon, Hexagon, or a straight Line) and the instances arrange themselves on it instead of sitting in a jitter cloud on the node's own position. Fill the shape's interior or ride its outline. The figure has its own animatable radius, centre offset, turn and pseudo-3D tilt/yaw, so a ring can grow, spin and lean over the document's frames. An Area swarm blends between an even ring layout and a random scatter on one Distribution dial; a Path swarm spreads evenly along the outline over whatever fraction of it you ask for, and its Progress dial wraps, so animating it past 1 rides the instances around further laps.

Instances can be turned to face outward or along the outline they sit on, and sized by their own index -- author that as a curve to taper the swarm from one end to the other, or as Min-Max to give each instance its own size.

Timing is three modes now. Stagger is what the swarm shipped with and stays the default. Window maps an instance's number to the moment it is born through a curve you author; FrameStep births one every N frames from a chosen first frame. In both, an instance is not drawn before its birth or after its death -- so the swarm builds up and clears, rather than merely being present the whole time -- and Die together clears the lot at one moment.

Everything defaults to an identity and the shape defaults to None, so a document you authored before this opens rendering exactly what it did.

Two places the port deliberately differs from Pyre, both recorded in the code. Even spacing over a full lap divides by the instance count rather than count-1: the outline is closed, so Pyre's divisor stacks the first and last instance on the same point. And the ordered Area layout uses one boundary-scaled ring construction for every polygon instead of Pyre's per-kind lattices -- it reads as ordered, it is not pixel-identical to Pyre's.

Two things are NOT in: the Custom hand-drawn polyline shape, and a preview overlay drawing the spawn figure/trace on the canvas. Both are named below as follow-ups with the reason.
