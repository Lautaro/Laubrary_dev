## Description

Effects become near-universal once three preconditions hold: the shape publishes the per-pixel sheets, the buffer can be padded so an effect may draw outside the shape (the owner ruled this is allowed), and the picture-rect call that tells an effect where the artwork sits inside a padded buffer is actually made - it is never called anywhere in Pyre today, harmless only because nothing pads. Roughly 13 to 20 of the 41 effects need those sheets; the rest are buffer-only. One distinction must NOT be flattened: some effects act on a single instance before it is composited with its siblings, others act on the finished buffer, and for overlapping swarms these give visibly different results because an effect can drop pixels. An earlier report claimed the stage could be collapsed with no behavioural cost - that claim is withdrawn. Present as one ordered list with honest greying-out, not four typed lists. Fix while here: one number (how far across this shape am I) currently means five different things across five files.

The authoritative design is the "Shaper - the design" tab of this group's planning document (also at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\SHAPER_THE_DESIGN.md). Read it before starting. Background: the three earlier reports are tabs of the PyrePlus group's planning document, and a condensed digest of them (incl. every withdrawn claim and every known live bug) is at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\P1-digest.md.
