Every asset edit was made as TEXT on disk followed by `AssetDatabase.Refresh`, never by saving a `SerializeReference` asset from a CLI eval (programme rule): these Zoe assets carry their whole reaction graph as managed references and a CLI save would have renumbered them.

The mechanism, for the record: `PyreChunksFx.Apply` (`Runtime/ZoetropePyre/PyreChunksFx.cs`) calls `ZoeLiveSampler.LiveSprite(ctx.Renderer)` and passes it as `sampleSourceOverride` into `ChunksFx.Burst`; `PaletteSplash.ResolveSprite(in ChunkModuleContext)` returns `ctx.SampleSourceOverride` when it is non-null and only then falls back to its own `sprite` field. `PaletteSplash.TrySamplePixels` reads that sprite's texture pixels directly — it does NOT multiply by the renderer's tint — so a `SpriteRenderer.color` tint would not have changed the spray at all. That is why the second character needed a genuinely different texture rather than a tint, and it is worth knowing before anyone tries to recolour a character by tinting it.
