## What

Every mechanical step between pressing play and a change arriving at a playing sound is verified by automated check. **That the result SOUNDS correct is not verified and cannot be by an agent.**

## What to listen for

- A chain with effects audibly differs from the same sound with the chain bypassed.
- Dragging a parameter while a sound plays changes what is heard, immediately, without a re-render.
- A delay or reverb tail is not chopped off at the end.
- A sequence plays its parts rather than a stale flattened mix of itself.
