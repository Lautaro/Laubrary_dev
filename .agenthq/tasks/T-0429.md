## Description

**Owner's ask, his words:** "The offset of the oscillator is hard to work with. Most of it will give extreme results that just is not useful. Figure out how to make it useful."

**Approach:** find the actual cause, not a cosmetic tweak. Establish how Offset combines with Amount, a binding's Depth and the target parameter's range; measure how much of Offset's slider travel produces a usable result; then choose and implement a mapping where the whole slider is useful, and write down why.

### 2026-09-27T15:21:05Z — agent — in_progress -> in_progress
Cause found, by measurement, and a fix in place (compiled, not yet committed or seen by eye).

CAUSE: Offset was an absolute amount added straight onto the oscillator's wave, in Amount's own units, on a slider running from -4 to +4, which is four whole swings either way. A binding then scales wave and offset together by its depth, so a modest-looking offset shoved the whole swing past the end of the parameter's range. It was also added after the strength curve, so a strength of 'none' still shoved the parameter.
Measured on a cutoff set at 1 kHz (middle of its slider), sweeping Offset across its whole range and reading from the engine how far the cutoff still moves and how often it sits pinned against an end:
- Shift, depth 0.25 (the default): 27% of the slider's travel usable; beyond about +/-1.25 the cutoff is pinned, up to 100% of the time.
- Shift, depth 1: 0% usable.
- Set, depth 1: 3% usable (only the very middle).

DESIGN CHOSEN: Offset now says which WAY the swing goes, not how far to shove it. Range -1 to +1: -1 swings only below the set value, 0 swings evenly both ways, +1 swings only above, and in between leans. The swing's reach is never more than Amount in either direction, whatever the Offset, so no Offset can push a parameter into its end stop further than the middle setting already does. The strength curve now scales the whole result, Offset included, so strength 'none' really leaves the parameter alone. This is how synthesiser LFOs usually offer 'bipolar vs unipolar', and every position of the slider now does something distinct and usable.

AFTER, same measurement over the new range:
- Shift, depth 0.25: 100% usable, never pinned.
- Set, depth 1: 100% usable.
- Shift, depth 1: at full depth the swing alone is already bigger than the parameter's range, so it pins 67% of the time even at Offset 0. That is Depth, not Offset. No Offset now pins it more than that (39-62% across the range, where the old Offset reached 100%).

NOTE FOR THE OWNER: an Offset saved under the old meaning is read under the new one. His current sound's 0.3 now means 'leans slightly upward', close to what it did; any old value beyond +/-1 reads as fully one way.
