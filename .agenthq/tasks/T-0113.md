## Description

Swarm becomes a modifier available on every generator. Two implementations behind one control: a generic wrapper that instantiates any generator N times with per-instance clocks, seeds, positions and parameter jitter, and a native path for generators that can do it more efficiently or want their instances to interact. Which one is in use must be shown, not hidden, because it changes what the controls mean. Two things needing real work rather than wrapping: the simulation-based generators, where N independent instances is an asymptotic blow-up rather than a linear cost (measured at roughly 200x cell updates for a realistic swarm), and the generators whose per-instance parameters do not exist yet. Also fix, during the port, that most big effects start every instance on the shared clock so instances pop in mid-animation instead of having their own lifetimes.

The authoritative design is the "Shaper - the design" tab of this group's planning document (also at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\SHAPER_THE_DESIGN.md). Read it before starting. Background: the three earlier reports are tabs of the PyrePlus group's planning document, and a condensed digest of them (incl. every withdrawn claim and every known live bug) is at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\P1-digest.md.
