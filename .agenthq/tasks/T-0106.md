## Description

A fill is attached to a shape node; the nearest ancestor owning a fill paints the whole subtree, and a child owning its own fill wins inside its own coverage. A fill emits albedo, optionally a height delta, optionally a veil that multiplies the shape's coverage but can never replace the shape's edge, and it must declare whether it composites over or adds. It reads only the fixed set of per-pixel numbers in design section B4; a fill needing a quantity the shape does not publish is greyed out WITH THE REASON SHOWN, never silently inert. First four fills: Solid, Gradient, Ramp-by-quantity, Texture. Note the existing Laubrary fill abstraction is used library-wide and is NOT to be extended in place - this is a new contract in the new namespace.

The authoritative design is the "Shaper - the design" tab of this group's planning document (also at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\SHAPER_THE_DESIGN.md). Read it before starting. Background: the three earlier reports are tabs of the PyrePlus group's planning document, and a condensed digest of them (incl. every withdrawn claim and every known live bug) is at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\P1-digest.md.

THE IDEA WORTH UNDERSTANDING. A fill only ever produces flat, unlit colour, plus optionally a statement of how high the surface is at that point. It never produces shine, gloss or shadow. That sounds like a limitation and is the opposite: it means when the lighting is built in a later task, every fill gets lit correctly without any of them knowing what a light is. Shine belongs to the lights, not to the paint.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A FILL ASKS FOR SOMETHING THE SHAPE HASN'T GOT. Nothing silently. A fill that needs to know, say, how hot the shape is, on a shape that does not report heat, refuses to attach and says so by name. The old tool's single most-reported confusion was effects that quietly did nothing, and that behaviour is now impossible here by construction.

KEY CONTRACT DECISIONS (full argument in FILL-CONTRACT.md). FC-1.5 the anchor: normalised coordinate = canvas point through the fill-owning node's root-to-node inverse, divided by that node's LOCAL support half-extents, recentred on the local box centre. The canvas box was rejected by measurement - it breathes 2.6736x over a full turn where the local box holds to 5.96e-8. FC-2.2 albedo has NO alpha channel (three floats); the veil is the sole transparency authority. FC-2.3/FC-2.3a linear non-premultiplied, sRGB decode at compile, ONE straight-alpha encode at the Color32 write. FC-3.2 the layer root always owns a fill and it cannot be removed, which makes the nearest-ancestor search total. FC-3.3 a Subtract member may not own a fill (it would paint whatever a later member puts back). FC-4.2 a bag's published set is the INTERSECTION over enabled Add/Intersect members. FC-4.4 an unavailable fill refuses to bind and records hasUnavailableFill/reason/node/count on the program, mirroring hasLeadingNonAdd exactly - the region falls to the nearest BINDING ancestor, never to nothing. FC-5.2 flat blittable op + static switch, not one managed object per tile.

SIX DEFECTS FOUND BY INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION BEHIND A FULLY GREEN AUDIT - the T-0105 pattern repeated exactly, and the reason this task ran a verifier and then a fix pass. (1) Multiplicative exclusivity c*(1-c) leaked the ancestor's colour into every antialias fringe - replaced with max(0, claim - descendantClaim), 119 -> 0/16384. (2) A NaN veil wrote NaN into every destination float silently, because Mathf.Clamp01 passes NaN through - 16384/16384 non-finite, now 0. (3) Over between EXCLUSIVE owners under-reported alpha: a 1px ring up to 25% transparent at every internal fill boundary, 244/16384, deficit 29.288. Fixed by per-owner subtree accumulators - an owner SUMS into its own accumulator (a partition), and Over appears only where a finished subtree folds into its parent. 244 -> 0/16384. FC-2.6a was amended: Over-vs-Add governs how the FINISHED layer meets what is beneath, never how one silhouette's own exclusive owners meet each other. (4) EncodePremultiplied premultiplied in linear then sRGB-encoded the premultiplied value - unreadable by any consumer, wrong by up to 67 codes at alpha 0.5. DELETED rather than repaired, because the conventional premultiplied byte for an additive sample is encode(rgb)*0 = 0, which loses the glow just as thoroughly; additive is carried by the float dst buffer or by compositing over an opaque backdrop first. (5) Every fill kind indexed the edgeDistance sheet including those declaring [None] - proved with a one-element array, fixed, 0/8 now. (6) TWO TESTS THAT COULD NOT FAIL: FT-5 read alpha out of PaintTile, which applies a SECOND clamp, so deleting the clause under test changed nothing; FT-9's allocation instrument was blind to 64KB and reported 0 gen-0 collections for 1MB of garbage. Both instruments replaced.
