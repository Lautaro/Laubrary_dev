## Description

### What

Every playing voice currently holds its own private copy of the sound's decoded audio. Ten overlapping copies of the same sound means ten copies of its samples in memory. The intended saving is one shared copy per cached sound.

### Why it is blocked, and it is a real block rather than caution

The private copy is wasteful but makes lifetime trivially safe: nothing is shared, so nothing can be freed while something is reading it. Sharing removes that guarantee. Clearing the engine's caches would then free audio a voice may still be reading, which is reading freed memory rather than an inconsistency.

Stopping a voice is a REQUEST that takes effect at the start of its next block. Asking every voice to stop is not the same as knowing they have stopped. **Sharing must not be attempted until stop-completion can be confirmed, not merely requested**, and no such barrier exists yet.

### Unblocks when

A confirmed-stopped barrier exists for every playing voice.
