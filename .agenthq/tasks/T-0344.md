Use case 1 (several Pyres, each with its own time, position, scale, alpha and tint) is buildable with workarounds. I built it from an empty Chunks window as four Pyre Blast cards, watched it play in the preview, and burst it in Play mode. The four explosions came out one after another, in the authored places, with the authored sizes, colours and opacities.

What gets in the way:
1. A Line or Ring pattern gives you 'several', but every point in it shares one tint and only draws random size and opacity from a band. The second one cannot be made 'small and orange'. Per-instance values mean one card per instance, and the window never says so.
2. There is no 'duplicate this card', so each extra Pyre repeats add, pick the same Pyre again, and set every value. That is about 30 of the ~54 actions.
3. The Offset pad shows no numbers, so you cannot read or type 'x = -3'.
4. On the stage, nothing ties a disc to its card. Single blasts are unlabelled, the disc takes its colour from the tint while its timing lane takes a different colour, and the card itself carries neither.
5. Tint multiplies onto the Pyre's own colours. A cyan tint on a red Pyre came out nearly black in Play mode, while the preview drew a cyan disc.

The timing surface behaves as designed: it is absent with one card, appears with the second, and the preview does not move. Its lanes can only be read; delays are typed on the cards.

Full friction log (16 items, 5 MAJOR, no BLOCKER), step count and captures are in WALK.md.
