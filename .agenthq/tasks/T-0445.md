### 2026-09-27T18:11:41Z — agent — in_progress -> in_progress
ESTABLISHED FROM THE ENGINE: an Envelope does not loop. It evaluates its curve once across the play (plus its extra time), then holds its last value for the rest of the play. It has no looping or periodic mode.
So the idle analyser was case (b): it restarted a simulated play every loop, back to back, so a one-shot envelope looked periodic. That is display behaviour misrepresenting runtime behaviour, which is exactly the owner's concern.

### 2026-09-27T18:18:37Z — agent — in_progress -> done
Done. The owner's instinct was right: this was display behaviour misrepresenting runtime behaviour.
- The Envelope modifier does NOT loop. It evaluates its curve once across the play (plus its extra time) and then holds its last value. The idle analyser was restarting a simulated play every loop, which made a one-shot look periodic. That is case (b).
THE CONVENTION NOW (explicit):
- While a sound plays: follow that play, unchanged.
- Idle, when something in the chain genuinely keeps running between plays (an oscillator set to Always, a timed step list with Retrigger off): plays are shown back to back, each started where those clocks have got to, exactly as successive real plays would be. The status line says 'looping plays back to back (each loop is a new play)', and the hover text says that anything that fires once per play is shown firing once in each of those plays.
- Idle, when everything fires once per play (an Envelope, a per-play oscillator or step list, a per-play Random): NOTHING moves. The lane shows one play's shape, and the bars show the moment the mouse points at on the lane (the start otherwise). The status line says 'one play's shape, nothing here repeats'.
VERIFIED BY EYE:
- An Envelope-only chain, in a temporary preview window (deleted afterwards): four captures over 1.5 s were identical, the dot sat at the start, and the status line was as above.
- The owner's real window: he has since added an LFO set to Always, so his chain correctly shows looping plays, with the 'each loop is a new play' wording.
