Done. Three separate things were wrong; the first alone explains "doesn't work at all".
1. IT NEVER DREW (root cause). The one-value-per-play draw lived in the native voice's start-up code, removed in f500b5fd. The new engine's start-up carried over the oscillator and step-list seeding but not Random, and the render only reads the stored value, so every play held 0.
   MEASURED BEFORE: 15 of 15 plays across his chain (Set) and test copies (Set and Shift) output exactly 0 for the whole play. Under Set that parks the parameter at the MIDDLE of its range regardless of Min/Max (his cutoff forced to slider 0.5 every play); under Shift it does nothing. That is the "weird things".
   FIX: drawn at play start in the engine's trigger seeding, which every path goes through (real plays, bounces/offline, analyser).
2. RANGE IN THE WRONG TERMS (same category as the Envelope Set fix and the LFO Offset fix). Min/Max ran -4..4, default 0.9..1.1: numbers that only meant something under Scale. Every binding mode reads a modifier's output as a -1..1 swing: under Set anything past +-1 clamps to an end, under Shift it overshoots the room and pins. His own earlier setting (Min -1.29) would have pinned the cutoff to 20 Hz on about 27% of draws; the default pins near the top under Shift or Set.
   FIX: Min/Max are now -1..1, default -0.25..0.25, with tooltips saying what -1/0/+1 mean under Shift and under Set. Saved values outside are held to -1..1 when drawn and displayed that way. (Under Scale an old 0.9..1.1 becomes 0.9..1.0; noted, not expected to matter.)
3. BIAS REVERSED. The draw raised to the power of Bias, so above 1 favoured Min, the opposite of its description. Now raised to 1/Bias. Measured mean of a 0..1 draw: 0.500 at Bias 1, 0.750 at 3, 0.248 at 0.33.
4. DISPLAY vs SOUND (the clock-desync category): the analyser's lane re-creates a play and would have drawn its own, different value. A re-created play now reuses the value the real play drew (recorded per sound name), and simulations never record. Between plays Random is play-fired, so it is held at none per T-0446.
