The three producer cards with the most authored shape now have their dials: Debris Scatter, Fragment Fracture, Palette Splash.

Debris Scatter is the deepest one — it leads with a Visual switch (Squares / Sprites / Sampled / Animated) that shows only its own mode's fields, then emission, flight, life/look with its size and alpha curves and a colour gradient, a Floor box you switch on from its own header, and — only when Sampled is picked — a sample-source picker, a tumble toggle, a Tint box, and the same pixel-modifier stack (tint, posterise, dither, dissolve...) the pre-capability-stack window offered, carried over onto this card unchanged. Fragment Fracture and Palette Splash are shorter and share a shape: a source (an animated one that outranks a plain sprite, for Fracture; a sprite that falls back to a sibling Fracture's own cut, for Splash), an Inherit-burst-direction toggle that reveals its own Direction dial only when switched off, then the same launch-speed/spread/gravity/drag/life packing every producer uses.

One thing worth knowing: none of these three cards has a Direction dial of its own on Debris Scatter — a burst's aim belongs to the recipe as a whole, and Debris Scatter's own field set (checked against the runtime class) never had a direction, only a Spread. Fragment Fracture and Palette Splash DO carry their own Direction, which only appears once you switch Inherit off, matching what their classes actually declare.
