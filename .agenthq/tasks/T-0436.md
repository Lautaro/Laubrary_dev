### 2026-09-27T16:30:08Z — agent — in_progress -> done
Done. The owner was right that 0.25 was odd, and the cause was the scale, not just the default.
WHY 0.25: it came in with T-0411, when Depth became 'a share of the parameter's whole slider'. Under Shift, depth 1 then meant 'swing a whole range each way', which from mid-slider pinned the parameter at an end two thirds of the time (measured: 67%). So 1 was unusable, and a quarter was picked as 'clearly audible without slamming into an end stop'.
CHANGE: Shift's depth is now a share of the ROOM the parameter has in the direction it is being moved. 1 = can travel all the way to that end, never past it; 0 = does not move. So Depth now reads 0 = none, 1 = full in all three modes (Set and Scale already did), and new bindings default to 1.
MEASURED: an oscillator on Shift at depth 1 moves a cutoff across the whole slider (0.000 to 1.000) from any starting point (tried 200 Hz, 1 kHz, 12 kHz). It only touches an end at the very peaks of the swing (3 to 5% of the time), instead of sitting pinned 67% of the time.
EXISTING SOUNDS: bindings saved before this keep their exact old behaviour; they are recognised by the version number stored with them. The owner's own binding (depth 0.22) is still read the old way, so his sound is unchanged until he touches that binding's depth or mode; it then switches to the new meaning, and the Depth tooltip says so beforehand.
Knock-on: the Offset sweep in T-0429 used the old Shift; under the new Shift nothing pins even at full depth.
**Proof of done:**

- [x] Owner's binding confirmed read the old way
