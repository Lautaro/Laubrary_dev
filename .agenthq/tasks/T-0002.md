## Description

Depends on T-0001 (the standalone `ZuiVectorEditor`/`ZuiDial` control must exist first). Add the right-click context card the user asked for, same spirit as ZuiEnvelope's own popover-driven editing, but built from general ZUI primitives since **ZuiEnvelope itself has no built-in settings card** (its own right-click just deletes a point — confirmed by reading `ZuiEnvelope.cs` directly this session, so don't go looking for a card mechanism baked into Envelope to copy wholesale; the card is caller-composed).

**Behavior wanted (verbatim from the user):**
- Right-click the vector's arrow → opens a context card containing a **dial** that lets you point the arrow and (if length is enabled for this instance, per T-0001's `allowLength`) adjust its length.
- The card should have toggles for showing/hiding **input boxes** and **labels** on the dial (same spirit as how Envelope's own context menu adds toggle options — use that as the UX reference even though the underlying mechanism is different).
- The vector's angle should be expressible in multiple modes: **local coordinate system**, **degrees**, or **radians** — the card should let the user pick.
- The card should have a **quantize** function: snap the dial to N discrete directions. 4 = N/S/E/W only. 8 = adds the diagonals (NE/NW/SE/SW). Should generalize past just 4/8 if that's free (the underlying `Cookbook2D.ToDir8`/`ToDir16` pattern suggests 16 is a natural next step, but 4 and 8 are the two the user explicitly asked for — build at least those correctly, extend further only if it's nearly free).

**Technical approach (from research this session):**
- Two general ZUI primitives are the right building blocks, found in `Zui.cs`:
  - `Z.Popover(anchor, build, options)` — general floating card, dismiss-on-outside-click.
  - `Z.Menu(anchor)` → `ZuiMenu` — a fluent builder ON TOP of Popover with **persistent Toggle/MiniRadio rows that stay open** when clicked — this is very likely the right base for "toggle input boxes on/off without closing the card."
- Wire the card's toggles to actually show/hide input-box fields and labels ON the dial control from T-0001 (so this task also means going back into `ZuiVectorEditor`/`ZuiDial` and adding the input-box/label sub-elements it can show or hide — that data-in-the-control-itself part may belong here rather than T-0001 if T-0001 didn't already build it; use your judgment on the cleanest split when you're actually in the code, this task owns "the card AND whatever the card needs to control").
- Quantize: use `Cookbook2D.ToDir8()`/`ToDir16()` (and add a `Dir4` enum + `ToDir4()` alongside them in `Runtime/Cookbook/Cookbook2D.cs` if it doesn't exist by the time you get here) to snap the dial's committed value. Off = free rotation (the default).
