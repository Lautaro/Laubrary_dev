Use case 4 (a particle spray based on a Zoe's sprite) is buildable with workarounds. One Palette Splash card makes a spray in Floating Disc's own colours that looks right in Play mode, both on its own and when fired from a Zoe's Death reaction (tested on a duplicate of Floating Disc; the owner's Zoe is untouched). The problems a user hits: the spray's picture can't be the Zoe itself — you must find a raw frame named 'cell_001'; the spray ignores the Zoe's live on-screen frame, so a Zoe-triggered spray always uses the authored frame; 'Spread 180' is only a half circle, although its tooltip promises a full one; the preview draws plain blue dots, so nothing shows the spray is based on the sprite; and on the Zoe, a Private Death row can't be switched to a library recipe — you have to add a second row. The report also answers whether wrapping Unity's Particle System would help: not for this case (it can't colour or order single particles by the sprite's pixels, and it breaks the one-clock preview), but it could be worth adding later as a separate producer for large-count effects.
