Solids render as lit 3D solids again. The geometry was never missing: ShaperSolids already built and rasterised real convex solids and published a per-sample surface normal, a facet-edge-line mask and a glow. What was missing was light, and three factory values.

T-0200 ruled that a document with no enabled lights renders unlit. For Silhouette that is right; for a Solid the shading term is not decoration on a shape, it is the only thing that expresses the shape. Unlit, a Box and an Orb are both one flat colour, and the white facet lines land on the white default fill and vanish. Solids are now exempt: an empty rig stands in a built-in key light, and the moment you add one real light it is gone entirely.

Three defaults were also wrong, and each was one of the things you listed as missing. Tilt shipped at 0 - these solids are orthographic and backface-culled, so a Box at yaw 0 tilt 0 presents exactly one face: not a box that looks flat, a box of which one flat square is genuinely all there is to see. Pyre has never shipped that pose. Edge glow and inner glow shipped at 0 against Pyre's 0.5 and 0.35, so 'no glow' was literally true out of the box.

**Details:**
The first build of the exemption used a DIRECTIONAL key and the contact sheet failed it outright. A directional light delivers one constant value across a flat face, so every facet solid came back with exactly as many luminances as it has visible faces - Box 2, Ring 2, Pyramid 2, Gem 8 - against Pyre's 119, 137, 155 and 173. That is the original complaint restated, not fixed; only the Orb survived, because a sphere's normal turns under the light on its own. What gradates a facet in Pyre is its POINT light's distance falloff, and Shaper's law already implements the identical 1/(1 + d^2/range^2). The key is now Pyre's own lamp.

A fourth defect surfaced only once the glows were on: the glow's COLOUR is decoded to linear at compile but the STRENGTH multiplying it was applied raw, so a linear colour was being scaled by a perceptual number. That washed every Solid out - darkest covered pixel at luminance 116 against Pyre's 31, and the same Orb that read 35..255 with the glows off read 116..255 with them on. The glow was not adding a halo, it was lifting the whole picture off its own black. Decoding the strength fixed it, and is exact for the default white glow.

Two differences from Pyre are left standing deliberately, both B8's 'consistent, not identical': Shaper shades in LINEAR and Pyre in gamma, so a Shaper shadow keeps its hue where Pyre's collapses to near-black; and Shaper's Gem defaults to 6 girdle sides against Pyre's 4, which is a shape choice from T-0155, not a lighting defect. Say the word on either and they change.
**Technical details:**
Exemption: ShaperLightCompiler.BindLayer sets scene.solidRigIsBuiltIn/.solidRig/.solidResponse only when prog.rig.count == 0 AND the layer's own receiveLighting is on. ShaperFillResolver.PaintTile derives solidBuiltIn (also requiring !add and compositeAlbedo == null) and LightSample selects rig+response at the single existing ShaperLightLaw.Shade call, so LT-1b's one-shading-site property holds. T-0200's gate in CompileResponse is UNCHANGED - it is per layer, and a layer may hold a Solid and an ordinary shape side by side, so relaxing it there would light both. Border path: bSolidHost/bBuiltIn carry the exemption per LR-5.4, still on the host's response and the host's normal.

BuiltInSolidKey (ShaperLightCompiler): Point, yaw -55 pitch 38 (Pyre.cs:351,352), distance 3.5*R range 4.7*R (Pyre.cs:357, PyreRenderer.cs:4260-4267) evaluated at R=20, colour 2.1 (Pyre.cs:362), specular 0.9 (Pyre.cs:363), ambient 0.05 (Pyre.cs:358). Fixed canvas position rather than per node: a per-node light is what LR-1.1 forbids. A solid moved far off-origin receives correspondingly less of it, which is what a real scene light does.

Defaults (ShaperSolids.cs): tilt 0->18 (Pyre gemTilt, Pyre.cs:341), edgeGlow 0->0.5 (Pyre.cs:1113), innerGlow 0->0.35 (Pyre.cs:1120); Compile's NaN fallbacks moved with them. Glow space: haloAmt/innerAmt now pass through ShaperSrgb.DecodeChannel in ShaperSolids.FillTile.

Cache identity needed no change: ShaperLayerKey.DocumentPart already reflection-walks the rig including ShaperLight.enabled, and the exemption is a pure function of rig.count.
