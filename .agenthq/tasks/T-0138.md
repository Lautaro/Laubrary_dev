**Details:**
Solids is a real, already-built shape generator in the engine, but nobody had ever added a way to author it in the mock editor. This adds that: pick a shape (Box, Pyramid, Can, Orb, Gem or Ring), and every dial that wouldn't do anything on that shape (e.g. 'Aspect' does nothing on a sphere, since a sphere looks the same from every angle) greys out and explains why in its tooltip, rather than just sitting there pretending to work or vanishing without explanation.

One important caveat: this mock had to make a placement choice that the REAL engine has not actually decided yet. Right now a Solid is shown as a 4th kind of shape node, sitting alongside the three the real engine already supports. But the real engine's own code only recognises 3 kinds today, and the team building it has not yet decided whether a Solid should become a 4th kind like this, get attached a completely different way, or live somewhere else in the document entirely. The mock says this plainly, right on the card itself, in a highlighted note -- so nobody mistakes 'this is how the mock demonstrates it' for 'this is the decided real answer'. That real decision is still open and needs the project owner's steer.
