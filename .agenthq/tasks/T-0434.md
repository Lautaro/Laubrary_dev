### 2026-09-27T16:01:01Z — agent — in_progress -> in_progress
CONFIRMED, by measurement and on screen, before changing anything. The owner is right.
Owner's settings at the time: Step, Per interval, 662 ms, Retrigger off, bound by Shift at depth 0.22 to a Low pass at 719 Hz; the sound plays for 0.77 s.
1. It is NOT the 'hold at the average' behaviour from T-0433 misfiring. That only switches on when something changes more than twice within one reading; here it is 1.5 changes a second x 0.34 s = 0.52, so it is off.
2. What the bars actually showed: NOT a live readout. They were a measurement of ONE simulated play, taken as a few readings each covering a third of a second, looped. For this 0.77 s sound that is only 3 readings (centred at 0.17, 0.34 and 0.51 s), so:
   - nothing after about half a second is ever read; the step change at 0.66 s falls outside every reading;
   - a 1 s step can never change inside a 0.77 s play at all;
   - the simulated play always starts the list at step 1, ignoring that with Retrigger off the list runs on its own clock and really does change every second.
   Across the 3 readings the bars moved 0.02 dB. On screen, four captures 0.6 s apart differ only by a one-pixel playhead line.
3. What the owner expects, a live per-frame readout of what the chain is doing right now, is a genuinely different thing, and possible. The third-of-a-second reading is needed to resolve the bass, but only while the chain changes during the reading. Freezing the chain at its current parameter values and measuring that frozen chain gives an exact reading of one moment, with full bass resolution. That replaces the looped measurement.

### 2026-09-27T16:19:13Z — agent — in_progress -> done
Done. The bars are now live: every frame they show what the chain does to each part of the sound at that instant.

HOW:
- Every redraw takes the modulated parameters' values at this moment. While the sound plays, they are read from the engine's own playing voice. While nothing plays, a silent display voice runs this chain's modifiers in real time, looping the play; running clocks (an LFO set to Always, a Step list with Retrigger off) are joined exactly as a real play would join them.
- The chain is then frozen at those values and its response measured. Freezing is what removes the old conflict: the bass needs a third of a second of audio to resolve, and a frozen chain does not change during it, so the reading is exact for that instant.
- Each response is cached per distinct set of values (a 3-step list needs 3 in total) and costs about 11 ms to take, at most one new one per redraw.
- The faint block behind each bar is now every level that band has reached since the last edit. The dot on each lane marks the value the bars are drawn at, playing or not.
- This replaces T-0433's 'hold at the average' behaviour, which no longer applies, since the bars are no longer averages.

VERIFIED:
- By measurement: for a still chain, the new instant reading matches the old play measurement exactly (0.000 dB). A display voice with a 1 s step list, Retrigger off, showed the step the clock says it should, 19/19 times over 3.5 s, changing exactly on the second.
- By eye, the owner's exact case (1 s step list, Retrigger off), in a temporary preview window on an in-memory sound (not his library; the window was deleted afterwards): the bars cycled through 3 distinct shapes, one per step, changing about once a second. Frames were about 0.22 s apart, so the on-screen intervals are coarse; the measurement above gives the exact timing.
- By eye, the owner's real window: at the time of the check all three of his steps were set to the same value, so the modulation was constant, and the still bars and flat lane are correct. Worth knowing when it looks still: identical steps mean nothing moves.
