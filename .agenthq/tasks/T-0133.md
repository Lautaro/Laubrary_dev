## Description

User feedback after reviewing the mock's screenshots: it's missing the section/toggle-bar bulk show-hide control that Pyre's editor has (`Editor/Pyre/PyreWindow.cs` `BuildSectionToggleBar()`, using the shared `Assets/Packages/Laubrary/Zui/Toolkit/ZuiSectionToggleBar.cs`) -- flagged as important for workflow. The design doc (SHAPER-UI-VISION-AND-DESIGN.md §I1) already named this control as 'free to adopt' since the window is ZuiSection-based throughout, but neither T-0130 nor T-0131 actually wired it in.

Add a `ZuiSectionToggleBar` to `Assets/ShaperMock/Editor/ShaperMockWindow.cs`, mirroring Pyre's pattern: store each top-level ZuiSection (Canvas, Light Rig, Layers) and each node-scoped section (Shape, Fill, Border, Swarm, Lighting response, Effects) as fields instead of locals, rebuild the toggle bar's roster whenever the section list changes (both a full window Rebuild() and a RebuildNodeBody()-only pass, since node-scoped sections come and go with node kind), and place the bar at the top of the window root per Pyre's own placement. Verify live via Coplay: bar shows/hides sections in Toggle Bar mode, solo (right-click) works, section identity survives a RebuildNodeBody() (e.g. drilling into a Bag member) without the bar losing track of which sections are open.

The Shaper mock editor now has the same quick show/hide bar the Pyre tool has at the top of its window, so you can collapse everything to just the parts you're working on instead of scrolling past sections you don't need right now.
**Details:**
How to use this: at the top of the Shaper Mock window there's a 'Sections / Toggle Bar' switch. Leave it on Sections and each part folds open/closed by clicking its own green header, same as before. Switch it to Toggle Bar and a row of buttons appears -- one per section -- click to show or hide that section, right-click a button to solo it (hide everything else), and right-click the 'Toggle Bar' switch itself to quickly cycle between your own arrangement, showing everything, and hiding everything. Your arrangement is remembered between sessions.
**Technical details:**
Assets/ShaperMock/Editor/ShaperMockWindow.cs: added ZuiSection fields for every top-level (canvasSection/lightRigSection/layersSection) and node-scoped (shapeSection/fillSection/borderSection/swarmSection/effectsSection) section, a toggleBarHost container placed at the top of root, and RefreshToggleBar() (throws away and rebuilds the ZuiSectionToggleBar instance, never just re-reads it) called at the end of BuildUI and at the end of RebuildNodeBody -- the latter matters because node-scoped sections are literally different C# instances after a drill/kind-switch, and a stale bar would hold dead references. Verified live via Coplay: roster correctly reflects the absence rule (Effects null for a Primitive), Toggle Bar mode's 8 buttons render, and the roster survives a RebuildNodeBody()-only pass without going stale.
