## Description

### What

A pooled audio source keeps its chain player between plays, deliberately — attaching a fresh one per play would add a component every time a sound started and never remove one. The consequence is that the player remembers only the MOST RECENT sound it was given.

If a sound is still finishing (a long reverb tail, say) when its audio source is recycled for a different sound, an editor edit aimed at the first sound is delivered to the second.

### Severity

Low. Nothing leaks and nothing crashes — the earlier voice is still owned and disposed correctly. The edit simply lands on the wrong sound. In practice a source is recycled when its sound is considered over, so it needs heavy overlapping playback plus live editing to hit.

### Why it was not fixed with the rest

The fix belongs to pooling policy, not to the chain: the pool must stop recycling a source whose voice has not finished. That is a change with its own blast radius and was deliberately kept out of the integration task.
