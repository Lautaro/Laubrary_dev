## Description

The fourth generator kind: a generator that produces a finished picture directly. Its contract is one line - it MUST publish coverage, and it MAY publish nothing else. With that it still groups, still masks, still swarms, still takes post-processing, and loses only a choice of fill (its picker shows one entry) plus the border stage and any effect needing shape-local position. Being monolithic must be a declared REASON, never a declared exemption, and there are exactly two legitimate reasons: the look is authored data rather than a rule (permanent), or nobody has split it yet (temporary, and the layer must say so). Depends on the Wave 1 contract check. Important correction to carry: no generator's colour and shape are mathematically fused - eleven were examined and in four the outline is completely indifferent to the palette, while in the rest the palette's transparency is MULTIPLIED into an edge rule the generator computed on its own.

The authoritative design is the "Shaper - the design" tab of this group's planning document (also at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\SHAPER_THE_DESIGN.md). Read it before starting. Background: the three earlier reports are tabs of the PyrePlus group's planning document, and a condensed digest of them (incl. every withdrawn claim and every known live bug) is at D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev\.agenthq\workspace\T-0098\P1-digest.md.

Built in the D:\UNITY\Laubrary Dev - Shaper worktree (feat/shaper, uncommitted, per instruction). Adds ShaperNodeKind.Composite (3rd node kind alongside Primitive/Bag) with a new ShaperOpKind.CompositeSample op: a generator's picture is baked once at compile time into a coverage raster, then sampled per-point and inverted (closed-form inverse of ShaperField.Coverage's smoothstep) into a pseudo-distance that flows through the SAME Combine/Sweep/Shell/Dilate fold every SDF primitive uses -- so grouping (Bag union/carve/intersect) works for free on hard combines. Border is refused structurally (EmitBorderJoin guard) and the fill is structurally locked (ShaperCompositeDef has no ShaperFillDef/ShaperFillKind field) rather than by convention. New bridge asmdef Runtime/PyreShaper (com.Lautaro-Arino.Laubrary.Pyre.Shaper) + Editor/PyreShaper keep Shaper's own asmdef Pyre-free (still only references ZuiRuntime); PyreFormCompositeSource hosts an existing PyreForm unmodified via IShaperCompositeSource. PyreCompositeCatalog classifies all nine (Inferno, Fork Blast, Orb, Torch, Arc Burst, Plasma Bloom, Jet, Radial Jet, Explosive Jet): 9/9 are reason-2 NotYetSplit (zero are AuthoredData -- none is baked/authored art, all are procedural); 4/9 (Orb, Torch, Arc Burst, Plasma Bloom) are palette-indifferent, 5/9 are palette-dependent (veil-multiplied), cited to exact file:line per generator.

Known, named gaps (see VERIFICATION.md's adversarial self-review): ShaperFillResolver.cs (1525 lines) is untouched, so a Bag containing a composite member doesn't yet downgrade its published quantity set to Coverage-only for its OWN fill's edgeDistance-reading modes (Gradient ByEdgeDistance / Ramp EdgeDistance) -- named as a real follow-up, not fixed here. Soft-combine (blend width > 0) with a composite member is untested and expected to degrade (the pseudo-distance saturates ~1 bake texel from the edge). Hosted PyreForm dials resolve to their static value only (no Curve/MinMax funnel -- that's PyreRenderer-internal). Two real asmdef-reference compile errors were hit and fixed during this pass (SpriteFx, Pyre.Forms.Kiln) -- verified via reflection probes, not just recompile_status, which was observed live in this session reporting "up_to_date" against a stale assembly.
