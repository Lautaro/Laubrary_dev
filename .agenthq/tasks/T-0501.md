## Description

Found by the T-0500 research pass (D:/Claude@GDrive/Zounds Non-Destructive Editing Research 2026-09-29.md), confirmed against the code by the ZPOC build (T-0491) on 2026-09-29. Pre-existing; not caused by ZPOC.

**What goes wrong:** a volume, pitch or time curve drawn on a Klip's waveform stores each point's position as a share of the trimmed region, and the engine reads it the same way. Moving a trim edge therefore moves every point onto different audio. A dip drawn under a drum hit is under something else after the trim changes.

**What already follows the same frame (update these together when fixing):**
- the waveform playheads map each play's source position in seconds into the trim window;
- the curve cards' playhead uses the play's progress through the region;
- the combined-result line on the waveform (T-0494) is laid out against the measured progress through the region.

All three are correct for the current model, so fixing the curves alone would put them out of step.

**Not decided (owner):** whether curves should keep their shape relative to the trim (today) or stay on the same audio. If they should stay on the audio, the options are:
- remap the points whenever a trim edit is made (no change to the saved format; one Undo step with the trim);
- store points in source seconds (a format change that needs a version marker, so old sounds keep sounding the same).

This overlaps the non-destructive editing discussion (T-0500), which the owner said not to build yet.
