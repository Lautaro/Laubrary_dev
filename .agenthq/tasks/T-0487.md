## Description

Owner: "When looper is on it needs to adapt to changes in fx chain or time, volume, pitch. It seems to sample the audio once and then use it for every loop."

MEASURED 2026-09-29 (in-memory Looper, 0.5 s loop, audio muted, reading the voice's own output 2-3 loop passes after each change):
- Effect parameter values: ALREADY live (level x0.25 for gain 1 -> 0.25).
- Klip volume: NEVER heard on a playing Looper (level unchanged).
- Klip pitch: NEVER heard (zero-crossing rate unchanged for pitch 1 -> 2).
- Chain structure (adding an effect): NEVER heard.
Cause: a play draws volume and pitch once at its start, and the chain layout (which also carries every curve shape and the time settings) is copied into the voice when it starts. Only effect/modifier parameter values, the loop region/crossmix and the authored speed are ever pushed into a playing voice. A one-shot hides this because its next play reads everything again; a Looper has no next play.

FIXED (part A): volume and pitch edits reach a playing sound (volume on every Klip, pitch on a Looper or live-speed sound). Kept check 19 ALL PASS.
OPEN (part B): chain structure, curve shapes and time settings on a playing Looper need the voice to be replaced; design proposed to the coordinator, not built.
