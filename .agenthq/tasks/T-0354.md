### 2026-09-16T20:39:19Z — agent — open -> done
Every card in a Chunks recipe now has an identity of its own.

- **Colour.** Each card has one colour, stored with the card, so it stays the same through a reorder. The colour shows in four places: a thicker left edge on the card, a chip in its header, its Timing lane, and the outline of its discs and rings on the preview.
- **Numbers.** The preview numbers every Pyre blast by when it goes off across the whole recipe, including single blasts. The card's chip shows the same numbers ("3", or "1–6" for a pattern).
- **Number chips on the stage.** Each number sits in a small chip in its card's colour and fades out with its disc. When chips would pile up (a tight ring, two blasts on one spot), a chip moves outward to a free spot.
- **Duplicate.** A card's header has a Duplicate button. It inserts a full copy right after the card, with its own id and colour, as one undo step.
- **Reorder by dragging.** Cards are reordered by dragging the ≡ grip. The ▲/▼ buttons are gone. The grip's tooltip says that a card's place is also its drawing order.
- **Lane names link to cards.** Clicking a lane's name in the Timing section scrolls to its card, unfolds it and briefly highlights it.
- **No more reflow.** A lone card now keeps an invisible Delay slot, so adding a second card no longer pushes the first card's fields around.
