## Handover Log

### 2026-09-29T15:54:57Z — agent — open -> open
Owner (2026-09-29), answering Q10: "I think each modifier could have a selected box and also an eye box. That affects if the visuals take its modification into account."

Decisions (made without asking, per the owner's standing instruction):
1. **Selected is a one-of choice.** At most one modifier is selected. Only its points can be grabbed. This replaces the time, then volume, then pitch click priority, which could grab the wrong curve.
2. **Eye off removes everything of that modifier from the picture:**
   - its contribution to the combined result line;
   - its random-point ellipses;
   - its dotted live (per-play) curve.
3. **Exception: a selected modifier with its eye off still shows its own editable points and ellipses,** because you cannot edit handles you cannot see. Its contribution stays excluded from the combined line, and its dotted live curve stays hidden.
4. **Both are view state, not saved data.** No Undo. They are kept per modifier instance, so they never drift when modifiers are reordered.
5. **Where they live:** on the modifier card header (a radio-style Select and a latching eye toggle), mirrored by the legend chips in the waveform view.

### 2026-09-29T16:26:29Z — agent — in_progress -> in_progress
Commit 93819edb.

**Built:**
- One-of selection (the pencils).
- Eyes on the waveform curves and on every modifier card (shared view state, per modifier object, no Undo).
- A combined-result line per shown curve: measured from the engine over a reference play, against source position, and drawn per pixel column when dense.

**Seen by eye:** a vibrato LFO added to a copy of the owner's Klip shows as a wobble around the pitch curve.

**Decision:** 'selected' lives in the waveform only (that is where points get grabbed). The chain cards carry only the eye.

**Deliberately not built yet (logged, not forgotten):**
1. Bands for per-play randomness (random draws, random points) over several seeds. The random-point ellipses and the live dotted curve already show that variation.
2. Adding arbitrary chain parameters to the waveform view on request.
3. Toolbar 'enable' toggles still create/enable the curve (not just show/hide). They are kept because they are the quick way to add a volume, pitch or time curve, and the eye now does show/hide.
4. A combined line for a value with no curve of its own (for example an LFO on pitch with no pitch curve). The Sound row shows it live instead.

**Not verified:**
- dense (per-column) drawing on a long sound has not been seen;
- the combined line's exact alignment with the audio at high pitch has not been measured.
