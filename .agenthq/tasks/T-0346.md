### 2026-09-16T20:14:06Z — agent — in_progress -> done
Use case 3 (a specific order of Pyres in specific positions over a timeline) is buildable with workarounds. I built four different Pyres as four cards, each with its own delay and position, in about 35 actions. The preview plays them in order at their places, and in Play mode they went off in the authored order at the authored spots.

What gets in the way:
1. The one timeline appears when it should and stays below the preview, but it is read-only. Dragging a band only moves the playhead, and a lane does not lead to its card, so every time change is a number typed on a card.
2. Positions are set on a small pad that shows no numbers and accepts none, so 'specific positions' are approximate and cannot be checked by eye.
3. Nothing on the stage shows the firing order for single blasts.
4. The lanes follow card order, not time. Lining them up means moving cards with up/down arrows (the UI rules require drag-and-drop), and that silently also changes which blast draws in front.
5. Every band is sized from a 0.6 s default while these Pyres really play 1.33 to 1.67 s, so the timeline misstates the overlaps.

10 frictions (6 MAJOR, no BLOCKER); details in WALK.md.
