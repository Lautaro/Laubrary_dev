1. BUG FOUND AND FIXED (43f6009d). The Klip handler started every real play with the SOUND's id as its play number, and that number seeds every per-play draw. So every play of a sound drew the same: random points did move off the authored curve, but identically on every play - no difference to hear between plays. My T-0483 checks missed it because they passed different play numbers to the voice directly; the real start path never did. The fork I took in T-0483 (drawing from a seed rather than storing a per-voice copy) was NOT the cause: the drawn curve does reach the audio (measured below); the seed was the same every play.
Same root cause also froze, per sound, a Looper's crossmix draws and the repeat / random-oscillator variation (all seeded from the play number) - fixed by the same change. Shared 'Always' random walks use their own name-based seed and are unaffected.
Fix: every play gets its own number (sound id shifted, xor a running count), so draws differ between plays and stay consistent within one.
MEASURED (kept check 17 section 5, ALL PASS; whole check ALL PASS): the old way, two plays of a sound rendered sample-identical (largest difference 0 - what the owner heard); consecutive play numbers give different seeds; the real start path (StartVoice on an audio source, twice) gives the voices different seeds; audible scale - a pitch curve whose ends may move +-12 semitones, two real play numbers: starts at 336 Hz and 628 Hz, predicted 333 and 638 Hz from each play's own draw (within 3 %), clearly different.
Owner-side causes also considered: tiny radii, or a curve not bound to anything, would also be inaudible - but the identical-plays measurement shows the seed was the real cause whatever the radius.
