FIX: ShaperWindow.Sections.cs, the Sweep box's SetHeaderToggle lambda, seeds extentDegreesDial->270 / extentFractionDial->0.75 ONLY when enabling AND the dial is still at its untouched identity value (Static mode, >=359.9 or >=0.999) -- an author who already dialled a real slice and toggled off/on keeps their own value.
