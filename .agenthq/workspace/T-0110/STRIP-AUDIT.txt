=== T-0110 fix pass, applied during this run (by the T-0110 agent, on top of a partial implementation found
already in progress from an earlier background research task that went beyond its research-only brief) ===

BUG FOUND AND FIXED: the reach/edgeCoverage formula's additive constant was 1, which put the transition's START
(not its END) at the reach depth -- so at the documented, authored DEFAULT reach=1 on a symmetric disc, the
shape's own deepest point (its centre) read edgeCoverage=0 (fully PLAIN), contradicting both B6 ("turn it to
maximum and it covers the WHOLE shape") and the code's own doc comments, which already claimed the opposite.
Measured before the fix (informal probe, not the committed audit): a 40px-radius disc at reach=1 (default),
sampled at (0,0): plain colour, height = commonHeightDelta * 0.25 (fully plain).
Fixed by changing the constant from 1 to 2 (ShaperFillOps.cs, the IndexedStrip case, `edgeCoverage` line) --
full derivation and the corrected formula are in STRIP-SPEC.md's inline comment at the fix site and in
STRIP-SPEC.md Part 7 (SS-7.4). Measured after the fix (same probe): centre reads the slot's own colour and its
full-weight height (see ST-2 above for the same property, now the committed regression test, using an even more
extreme reach so the property holds by a comfortable margin rather than exactly at the boundary).
