########## LT14b_OrbSilhouetteIsRotationInvariant
LT-14b Orb's silhouette is rotation-invariant, its shading is not
  alpha samples differing across the rotation: 0/16384 (expected 0)  PASS
  RGB samples differing across the rotation: 3640/16384 (expected >= 100)  PASS
  This generator rotates the published NORMAL forward rather than rotating the light
  backward as Pyre does (PyreRenderer.cs:4442-4455) - it is the other side of Pyre's own
  identity N.(Rot^-1.L) == (Rot.N).L, and it is forced by LR-1.1: a per-particle inverse-
  rotated light IS a light owned by a generator, which LR-1.1 forbids outright.
  RESULT: PASS
