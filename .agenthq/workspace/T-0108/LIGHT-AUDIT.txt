  authored direction, while an Orb's N = P/R is unit only to float precision. The two
  providers therefore differ in the last bit at 112 of 512 cases
  (worst 1.2e-7), and a pow(nh, 24) specular amplifies that to ~5e-6 in the output. The
  exact assertion that IS available is leg (ii): given the same bits in, the same bits
  out, every time. LR-3.5 is the clause worth keeping; LT-1's wording is the one to fix.
  the 64 normals are GEOMETRY-DERIVED (an Orb's N = P/R at 64 covered samples spread
  across the disc), so the Constant provider is driven to values it did not choose - a
  self-consistent constant would make this leg vacuous.

  agrees. LT-1b is the half that makes 'one law' falsifiable, and LT-16 is the half that
  makes the shared FRAME falsifiable.
